## Overall Impression

Functionality is not the problem; placement is. Every time users said they could not find something, the answer was one more icon (Account capacity, Reset calendar, Skill activity, Explore integrations in the last two days). A stripe button's position no longer tells what it opens: a docked column, a panel floating over the canvas, a centred modal in one of nine widths, or a full-screen blurred overlay. The single biggest opportunity: one rule for the stripes and two contracts for what opens.

## What's Working

1. The waiting signal: amber means only waiting; chip, names, W and the list sorted by wait agree on desktop and phone.
2. Accessibility engineering of the chrome: APG toolbars with roving focus, aria-expanded vs aria-haspopup, focus hand-over across the 640 px switch, a skip link.
3. The Settings dialog itself: accessible search, consistent rows with Details, a note per section.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The stripes are a wall of 13 unlabeled icons where position predicts nothing.**
Why: users must recall each glyph; left groups by topic, right by kind; "Right panels" holds 6 dialogs out of 8; groups have no names (EdgeRails.tsx:195).
Fix: one rule — groups follow the user's question, ≤3 per group, same dividers on both stripes, each group named for screen readers; a press says what kind of thing it opens. Option: fold the occasional records (history, tools, skills, capacity, calendar) behind one labeled menu reusing the dock's More (reverses the owner's 2026-10-22 order — owner decision).
Command: /impeccable layout, /impeccable distill.

**[P1] Four panel models behind identical buttons.**
Why: Sessions/Accounts/Detail dock; Usage/Machine float over the canvas and the minimap (with list + detail + Usage open, ~420 px of canvas at 1440; the known defect #847); Radio is declared a panel (aria-expanded) but opens an aria-modal dialog with a backdrop blur that DESIGN.md (#882) forbids.
Fix: panel = docked column (canvas fit accounts for it, never covers the minimap); dialog = one header component and three size tiers; Radio becomes a dialog without blur.
Command: /impeccable layout.

**[P1] Dialogs share no house style.**
Why: 9 widths, 3 title systems, header heights 37–94, close buttons 24–36 px at 15–25 px insets; Tool activity is a 1296×810 modal holding a three-line empty state.
Fix: one dialog header (sans title, one-line note, actions, ×), S 560 / M 820 / L 1120, height follows content up to 82vh.
Command: /impeccable polish.

**[P2] One subject, five homes.**
Why: Claude quota appears in Accounts, Usage, Account capacity, Reset calendar and Quota history; three period vocabularies (today/month/all, 7d…90d, 24 hours…90 days); feature settings in three patterns.
Fix: one home per subject, the rest as links from existing readings; one period set.
Command: /impeccable distill, /impeccable clarify.

**[P3] Settings is grouped by implementation; the phone's More is 10 flat 30 px rows.**
Fix: caption the existing nav groups (Deck / Alerts / Connections / Data & privacy / About); More uses the desktop's groups with 44 px rows.
Command: /impeccable adapt.
