## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** mixed.

The interaction and copy are authored for this product:
- the waiting row quoting Claude Code's own sentence;
- the "N waiting" jump to the longest-blocked session;
- the 60-second activity chart on each card, on one shared scale;
- a Clear dialog computed from what the server will destroy;
- "Restart anyway" with its consequence;
- measurement copy that says "numbers paused" or "unpriced" rather than guess.

The visual skin is interchangeable:
- Tailwind's 300 row on near-black (`styles.css:58-63`);
- 10px-radius soft-shadow panels;
- the system font stack;
- stock React Flow controls and minimap;
- a gradient logo with a cyan halo.

The biggest missed opportunity is the overview canvas. It should be the signature, and at the zoom real boards settle into it is a field of 80px boxes with 4px text.

**Deterministic scan:**
- The CLI found 14 hits: bounce-easing ×9, layout-transition ×4, side-tab ×1. They are all in `styles.css`, plus one in a test. The TSX and HTML markup has 0 hits.
- The browser overlay found 416 hits page-wide: dark-glow 126, bounce-easing 122, ai-color-palette 38+, undersized-ui-text 99, gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow 22, layout-transition 12, plus smaller counts.

How the detector and the review line up:
- **They agree.** undersized-ui-text backs the legibility findings (#846, #857, #858, #859), and ai-color-palette plus dark-glow on the logo and the canvas corners back "interchangeable skin" (#867, #868).
- **False positives.** State-colour glows on in-flight and error bubbles, semantic family palettes, the canvas clip, "roboto" (the body is the system stack), and `stroke-width` read as a layout property.
- **B and C disagreed.**
  - B claimed the quota and context bars keep moving under reduced motion. That is wrong: `styles.css:8289-8293` covers them, and #863 was corrected before filing.
  - C judged the bubble overshoot (#860) and the prompt side stripe (#862) intentional taste calls. They were filed anyway, noted as taste calls, so the decision is made on purpose.

## Overall Impression

The deck's behaviour is authored and honest. "Something needs you" is designed from start to finish, and the Clear and restart flows are models of consequence-first copy. The primary surface fails at the moment of use:
- the overview canvas is illegible;
- the right-hand rails cover it;
- inspecting an agent is hidden behind `D`;
- canvas cards show no focus ring.

The single biggest opportunity: make the overview a legible board, and make selecting a node inspect it.

## What's Working

1. The one irreversible action is honest. `clear-confirm.ts` builds its copy from `GET /api/clear` and relabels the button to match, and `ClearConfirm.tsx` focuses Cancel. The restart banner speaks the same way.
2. The needs-input path is complete:
   - the amber waiting row;
   - longest-blocked-first sorting;
   - the "N waiting" jump;
   - an always-mounted polite live region (confirmed live: "<session> is waiting for your permission.");
   - Codex sessions say "we have no signal" rather than pretending.
3. The engineering floor is high:
   - about 400 guard tests (contrast floors, dead CSS, focus trap, landmarks, reduced-motion reach);
   - 17 dialogs, all named, with Esc and focus return;
   - a deliberate reduced-motion CSS contract with no global kill;
   - 0 unnamed controls out of 84 tab stops.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Clicking an agent opens nothing, and the details rail is mouse-unreachable once closed (#814; see also #817, #845)**
- *Why it matters:* inspecting an agent is a primary job, and the tour promises a click does it.
- *Fix:* open the rail on node click and close it on deselect; add a Details control on the ribbon; keep `D`.
- *Command:* /impeccable onboard

**[P1] The overview canvas is unreadable (#846)**
- *Why it matters:* at zoom 0.316, 12px text draws at 4px and cluster labels at 3.2px.
- *Fix:* floor auto-fit at a readable zoom; draw counter-scaled glance labels at 11px or more; never shrink cluster labels below 1×.
- *Command:* /impeccable layout

**[P1] The right-hand rails float over the canvas, and the shell has no breakpoints (#847, #849, #848)**
- *Why it matters:* 148px of canvas is left at 1024px, and none at 640px (200% zoom). At 400px the page overflows 230px and the machine rail is unreachable.
- *Fix:* grid-track rails, one tabbed rail when narrow, a topbar overflow menu.
- *Command:* /impeccable adapt

**[P1] The pairing fingerprint check cannot be performed (#815)**
- *Why it matters:* the dialog asks the user to compare fingerprints, and no screen shows this deck's own.
- *Fix:* show it, with copy, in "This deck on the network" and the Local network header.
- *Command:* /impeccable harden

**[P1] Canvas cards show no focus ring (#869)**
- *Why it matters:* React Flow's `outline: none` (0,3,0) beats the global ring.
- *Fix:* a `.react-flow__node:focus-visible > .agent-node` ring, plus a guard test.
- *Command:* /impeccable harden
