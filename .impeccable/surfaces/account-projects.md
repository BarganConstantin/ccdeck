pricing, range filters, refresh, clipboard and error behavior.

one 12px scanning stroke with a 1600ms indeterminate cadence. It pauses on
hidden documents and is static under Reduce Motion. Never show project names,
counts, dollar amounts or measured percentages until the report provides them.

loader open. Existing reports stay visible when a new period is requested.
No added dependencies or minimum waiting time. Initial loading, success,
error, refresh and responsive/reduced-motion behavior are verified in browser.
