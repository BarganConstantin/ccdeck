## Product semantics preserved

- Provider-reported timestamps remain observations, not guaranteed reset events. No weekly recurrence, fake schedule or new countdown timer.
- Upcoming counts are fresh in-week reported weekly resets. Fresh-data counts are in-week schedules requiring a reading. Other covers unknown, elapsed and later times, and can also contain stale readings.
- Source-marked stale and older-by-age readings remain excluded by the same conditions. Only note wording changed; a recent fetch is not silently promoted to reliable when marked stale.
- Usage means reported capacity **used**. The existing warning threshold at 70% indicates less remaining capacity; it does not establish a technical account error or failed login. Unknown usage stays unknown. No success color promises availability.
- Local timezone/date formatting and existing clock, roster refresh, failed-read retention, sorting, day selection, account views and both capacity windows are preserved.
